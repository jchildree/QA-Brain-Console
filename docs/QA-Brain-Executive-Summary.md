# QA Second Brain -- Executive Summary

**QA Brain** is a personal quality-management drafting and analysis tool built on Obsidian, a note-taking application. It is not a replacement for Intellect, our validated Electronic Quality Management System (EQMS) -- it's a working layer that sits in front of it. Incidents, CAPs, audits, change requests, and training records get drafted, cross-referenced, and analyzed here first; once a record is finalized and approved, it gets entered into Intellect as the official system of record. Think of it as a scratchpad and search engine for quality data, not the filing cabinet itself.

Where it earns its keep is in the work that happens *before* a record is finalized -- work that today mostly lives in scattered Word docs, email threads, and memory. Preparing for an audit means pulling every open CAP or incident that could be a finding, in minutes instead of digging through folders. Investigating a deviation means being able to ask "what else have we seen like this" and get every related incident, its root cause, and its corrective action pulled up at once, instead of relying on someone remembering it happened before. And because every save is version-controlled, there's a full revision history and audit trail on every draft -- who changed what, and when -- before it ever reaches Intellect.

The AI in QA Brain is two off-the-shelf Obsidian plugins, not a custom-built model: Copilot, which provides chat and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over everything in the vault, and Smart Connections, which adds semantic search so related records surface even when they don't share exact keywords. In practice that means asking things like "summarize root causes across all CAPs tagged contamination" or "what are the most common root causes across all incidents this quarter" and getting an answer pulled directly from the actual records, with sources, rather than having to read through every file by hand. It's the same category of tool as any AI chat assistant layered on your own documents -- nothing proprietary, nothing that touches Intellect or any validated system, and nothing that makes decisions on its own.

Worth being explicit about the boundaries. QA Brain is not a validated system, is not connected to Intellect, and does not replace any GMP-required documentation or approval workflow -- nothing here is "official" until it's re-entered into Intellect through our normal process. It's a personal tool, running on my machine, used to draft faster and think more clearly before work goes through the real system. The AI never approves, signs, or finalizes anything; it summarizes and searches records I've already written, and every summary still gets checked against the source record before it's acted on.

Underneath, it's organized around seven record types that mirror how we already think about quality events: SOPs, Incidents, CAPs, Change Requests, Audits, Training records, and Meeting Minutes, plus a free-form Notes area for early drafting. Every record gets a unique ID (like INC-20260528-001) and moves through the same four-stage lifecycle -- Draft, In Review, Approved, Obsolete -- so at a glance I can tell what's still being worked and what's settled. Records link to each other directly: an incident points to the CAP it spawned, the CAP points back to the incident and any related audit or training record, so the chain of "what happened, why, and what we did about it" is traceable in one place instead of split across systems.

In short: QA Brain is a faster, better-organized way for me to draft and analyze quality records before they become official -- built on existing off-the-shelf tools, with AI that searches and summarizes but never decides. It saves time on audit prep and root-cause work; it doesn't touch, replace, or change anything about how Intellect operates as our validated system of record.
